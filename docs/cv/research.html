Research Projects

Adaptive ControlNet Stopping · exploratory research experiment Jul 2026
  • Asked whether ControlNet guidance is still needed once structure has formed: Stable Diffusion 1.5 + Canny ControlNet, DDIM, 30 steps, comparing full control, fixed stop, gradual decay and a training-free adaptive stop.
  • Designed stopping signals from edge-F1 consistency, low-frequency latent change, residual magnitude and trend, high-frequency residual share, rolling slope and drop-from-best; added counterfactual oracle labels and a sequential router.
  • 60 reference generations (30 cases × 2 seeds) under safety limits of edge-F1 loss ≤ 0.02 and CLIP loss ≤ 0.01. The oracle needed 27.5 steps on average, while the held-out router conservatively chose step 29, showing room to stop earlier that the router has not yet captured.
Qwen2.5-VL Fine-Tuning on MMMU · multimodal learning trial Jun 2026
  • LoRA fine-tuning of Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct to generate exam-style questions from diagrams (MMMU Accounting, Biology, Chemistry; 20 samples each), r = 16, α = 32, 30 steps on a Tesla T4.
